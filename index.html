    About this devotional

    Each devotional is drawn from a single message preached at Jewel City Church, Gombe, and stays wholly within that message’s own words and scriptures: an anchor scripture, a short reflection with its references, a question to carry through the day, and a closing prayer. A new edition is published every midnight, West African time.
